# workflow (brainstorm)

1. State the goal and the constraints in two lines (4 GB VRAM, one file, no dependency, must work offline...). A wrong
   constraint spoils every option, so say them out loud.
2. List 3-5 options that differ in kind: build, reuse something installed, buy/ask for it, do it by hand, do not do it.
   "Do nothing" and "use what exists" are always on the list (ponytail ladder).
3. Per option, one line each: what it costs, what it gives up, the cheapest check that would kill it. Mark what is
   measured and what is guessed (knows-unknowns).
4. Kill the dominated ones, out loud and briefly.
5. Recommend one, with the reason, the runner-up, and the fact that would flip the choice. Confidence on the fixed scale
   (calibration). Then go, unless it is costly or hard to undo - then offer the options and wait.

Brainstorming other people's products: take the core idea of each, not the feature list (what problem it solves, the
smallest mechanism that solves it), and write the mechanism in one sentence before copying anything.
